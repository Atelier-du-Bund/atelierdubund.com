How It Works

Four quiet steps to a suit that is yours.

01

The invitation

We receive you by appointment, in a private Montréal atelier — an unhurried conversation before a single measure is taken.

02

The measure

Your proportions and posture are read entirely by hand, then cloth, lining and finishings are chosen together.

03

The making

Your suit is cut and hand-finished in the Shanghai–Hong Kong tradition, each stage overseen personally.

04

The homecoming

A final fitting, in person, in Montréal — and a suit you will return to for years.
